curator's note

Happy Birthday, my love.

Are you smiling? Of course you are.
When it hit me a few days back that, oh, it's September. And it's your birthday month. All I was thinking was, how do I make it special for you? Because just like each year, we couldn't meet this year as well.
And just like each year, all I wish is that this world becomes a better place for you to live in.

We have seen our ups and downs. We've seen each other change, grow, get lost, find our way back, and somehow, through all of it, we're still here.
And I think that's what makes you so special to me. You're not just someone I have good memories with. You're someone who has become a part of my life in a way that I don't think I can properly explain.

On your birthday, I wish all the people around you stay healthy and happy. I wish you happiness that doesn't depend on anything or anyone. I wish you courage for the difficult days and enough madness to enjoy the good ones.

I love you.
And yes, you better be smiling right now.
Happy Birthday Baby❤️
